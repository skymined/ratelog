Devin Desktop (formerly Windsurf) — Teams pricing structure clarified

Resolved earlier conflicting reports about Teams pricing: it’s a hybrid model, not a flat per-seat rate — $80/mo minimum team spend, $40/mo per full seat (Pro-equivalent quota + Desktop access), and unlimited free flex seats (shared credits, no Desktop access).

2026-06-01• changed

GitHub Copilot — Flat premium-request quotas replaced with AI Credits

GitHub replaced its flat monthly Premium Request quotas (e.g. 300/month for Pro) with a token-consumption-based "AI Credits" system (1 credit = $0.01) across all plans, and introduced a new top individual tier, Copilot Max, at $100/month. Total credit allowances ended up higher than sticker price once bonus credits are included, but billing is now metered rather than a flat predictable count.
