What’s changed

2026-06-01↑ improved

Teams split into two seat tiers

Standard keeps its $40/seat price but gains materially more included usage, split into first-party and third-party model pools. A new $120/seat Premium tier (5× Standard’s usage) was added for heavy daily users.

source →
2025-08-12• changed

Teams moved to usage-based billing

Teams moved from a fixed per-request cost (250 included requests/month/seat, $0.08 per extra request) to variable, API-rate usage billing.

source →
2025-06-16• changed

Pro moved off flat "500 fast requests"

Cursor dropped the old flat ‘500 fast requests/month’ Pro model for usage-based pricing: Pro now includes at least $20/month of inference at API prices and unlimited use of the 'Auto' model. Also launched the $200/month Ultra tier.
