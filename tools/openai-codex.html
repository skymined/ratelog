What’s changed

2026-08-06↑ improved

Free-tier chat cap removed

GPT-5.6 Luna became the default Free-tier model, and OpenAI began removing the numeric cap on Free-tier text chats entirely, moving toward "unlimited" (subject to abuse guardrails).

2026-04-02↑ improved

Business seat price cut

Cut ChatGPT Business per-seat price about $5: from $30/mo ($25 annual) down to $25/mo ($20 annual).

2026-04-02• changed

Codex switched to token-based credits

Switched Codex from flat per-message billing to token-based credits at API rates for Plus, Pro, and Business — cost now scales with tokens used instead of one flat unit per task.
