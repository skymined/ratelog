JetBrains AI Assistant

by JetBrains · official site →

JetBrains' AI layer across its IDEs (and the Junie coding agent), billed through a credit system where 1 AI Credit ≈ $1 of underlying model usage.

Last change: ↑ Org credits extended to 12-month validity · 1 month ago
plans

Every tier

Free tier: AI Free — 3 AI Credits per 30 days (no top-ups). Local/on-device completions are unlimited and don’t use credits. Unavailable on free Community-edition IDEs.

AI Pro (Individual)
$10/mo
10 AI Credits per 30 days (1 credit ≈ 10 AI Chat requests, 40 in-editor completions, or 25 explanation requests). Resets 30 days from activation, not your billing date.
Individual professional developers
AI Ultimate (Individual)
$30/mo
35 AI Credits per 30 days ($30 plan + $5 bonus), or 420 credits/year on the annual plan. Junie’s agent burns credits much faster than chat — each turn is a long multi-step request.
Individual power users / heavy Junie agent use
AI Pro (Org seat)
$20/mo
20 AI Credits per 30 days per seat, same $1=1 credit mechanics.
Teams licensing seats centrally
AI Ultimate (Org seat)
$60/mo
70 AI Credits per 30 days per seat.
Teams needing Ultimate-level capacity
AI Enterprise
$60/mo
Quota described only as “on par with AI Ultimate or higher” — exact number not yet published; JetBrains says the enterprise quota model is still being finalized.
Large organizations (org-only)
history

What’s changed

2026-07-07↑ improved

Org credits extended to 12-month validity

Announced transitioning org/business AI licensing from fixed seat quotas toward on-demand credits with validity extended from 30 days to 12 months, rolling out through July–August 2026.

source →
2025-08-25↓ reduced

Quotas tied to plan price, shrinking for heavy users

Replaced its opaque per-plan quota with a rule that each plan's AI-Credit allowance equals its USD price (plus a small bonus on Ultimate). JetBrains acknowledged quotas were shrinking for a meaningful share of users, especially heavy Junie agent users, since old quotas had been set unsustainably high as a promotional measure.
