Warp

by Warp Terminal Inc. · official site →

An AI-native terminal that open-sourced its client (AGPL-3.0 / MIT) in April 2026 while keeping its Oz cloud-agent orchestration platform proprietary, priced on a credit-metered model.

Last change: ↓ Free tier’s onboarding credit grant no longer listed · today Context window: 1M tokens (Claude Sonnet 5)
plans

Every tier

Free tier: Free — pay-as-you-go/BYOK only. Warp’s own pricing FAQ states the Free plan "doesn’t include bundled AI usage for the Warp Agent"; you bring your own API key or pay per-request at provider rates. No onboarding credit grant found on the live pricing page.

Build
$20/mo
1,500 AI credits/month (~$20 of usage at API rates), refilling every 30 days; unused monthly credits don’t roll over, but purchased “Reload” credits roll over up to 12 months.
Individual professional developers
Business
$50/mo
1,500 AI credits per seat/month, same refill cadence as Build, plus a shared rollover pool of purchased reload credits.
Teams up to 25 seats
Max
$200/mo
18,000 AI credits/month — 12× Build’s allotment, same refill cycle.
Power users / heavy individual AI usage

2026-08-23↓ reduced

Free tier’s onboarding credit grant no longer listed

The live pricing page and pricing FAQ no longer mention a 150-then-75 monthly credit allowance for Free users; Free is now described as BYOK/pay-as-you-go only. No dated changelog entry found for when this changed — noting the date we confirmed it.

Legacy tiers collapsed into one $20/mo plan

Collapsed three legacy paid tiers (Pro, Turbo, Lightspeed) into a single $20/mo Build plan; independent analysis characterized this as a net price increase for many existing $10–15/mo subscribers, even though per-unit overage pricing improved and BYOK access was added.
